### Introduction

Advanced cutaneous squamous cell carcinoma (cSCC) represents a significant clinical challenge. In this article, we dive into the structural biology, mechanism of action, and pivotal trial efficacy of cemiplimab (Libtayo®), a fully human IgG4 monoclonal antibody that has transformed the management of cSCC.

### 1. Mechanism of Action and Structural Biology

Cemiplimab is a high-affinity anti-PD-1 monoclonal antibody. By selectively binding to the Programmed Cell Death Protein 1 (PD-1) receptor on T-cells, cemiplimab blocks its interaction with the ligands PD-L1 and PD-L2. This pathway blockade restores T-cell activation and enhances anti-tumor immune responses.

### 1.1 Structural Biology Insights (PDB 7WVM & 8GY5)

* Affinity: Exhibits a binding affinity (KD) of approximately 0.60 nM.
* Binding Epitope: Structural analysis using PDB coordinate structures [7WVM](https://www.rcsb.org/structure/7WVM) shows that cemiplimab binds to the front face of the PD-1 IgV domain.
* Glycosylation Dependency: Cemiplimab binding is heavily dependent on the glycosylation status of the N58 residue on the PD-1 receptor. Deglycosylation or mutation at N58 reduces binding affinity by over 10-fold, highlighting a highly specific glyco-epitope.

### 2. Efficacy in Pivotal Trials

### 2.1 Advanced cSCC: EMPOWER-CSCC-1 (NCT02760498)

The pivotal Phase 2 trial evaluated cemiplimab across three advanced cSCC cohorts:

* Metastatic Cohort (3 mg/kg Q2W): Achieved an Objective Response Rate (ORR) of 50.8%.
* Metastatic Cohort (350 mg fixed Q3W): Achieved an ORR of 42.9%.
* Locally Advanced Cohort (350 mg fixed Q3W): Achieved an ORR of 44.9%.
* Pooled Outcomes: Across all 193 patients, the pooled ORR was 47.2%, with a Complete Response (CR) rate of 16.1% and a median Progression-Free Survival (PFS) of 26.0 months.

### 2.2 Adjuvant Setting: C-POST (NCT03969004)

The randomized Phase 3 trial evaluated adjuvant cemiplimab vs. placebo in patients with high-risk resected cSCC:

* Disease-Free Survival (DFS): Adjuvant cemiplimab reduced the risk of recurrence or death by 68% (Hazard Ratio = 0.32 [95% CI: 0.20–0.51], P<0.0001).
* 12-Month DFS Rate: 92.4% in the cemiplimab group vs. 69.5% in the placebo group.

### Conclusion

Cemiplimab’s high binding affinity and glyco-epitope specificity translate into robust, durable clinical responses in both advanced and adjuvant high-risk resected cSCC.
